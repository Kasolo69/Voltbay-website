From Proposal to Power: How It Works

We manage the engineering, capital expenditure, and compliance from start to finish.

Step 01

Site Assessment

We evaluate your parking layout, driver traffic flows, and available grid connection capacity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Volt Bay.

How much does installation cost my property? ▼

For qualifying partner sites, Volt Bay may fund the charging equipment and standard installation, subject to site feasibility, commercial approval, and the final partnership agreement.

How does the revenue share work? ▼

Revenue-sharing structures are discussed during the proposal process, backed by transparent reporting detailing kWh delivered and session volume.

What happens to the chargers during load shedding? ▼

Our chargers feature intelligent power management to protect equipment and optimize charging schedules. Optional battery or generator backup can be integrated where technically feasible and commercially agreed upon with the site owner.

What commitment is required in the partnership agreement? ▼

Agreement structures are tailored to each site and may include an initial pilot phase where appropriate.

Who handles equipment maintenance and repairs? ▼

Volt Bay provides ongoing routine servicing, remote firmware updates, and technical maintenance as set out in the partnership agreement.

Which vehicle types can use Volt Bay chargers? ▼

We deploy high-power DC fast chargers equipped with industry-standard CCS2 connectors, compatible with modern passenger and commercial EVs in South Africa.
